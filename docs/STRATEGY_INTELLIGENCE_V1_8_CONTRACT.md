# v1.8 Strategy Intelligence Contract

v1.8 consumes a v1.7 adaptive decision context and ranks strategy candidates.

Flow:
v1.6 Outcome Patterns
→ v1.7 Adaptive Decision Context
→ v1.8 Strategy Intelligence
→ advisory strategy ranking
→ governed decision layer

Scoring is a deterministic prioritization heuristic, not probability or guarantee.

v1.8 may:
- rank strategy candidates
- lower ranking confidence
- hold for more evidence
- surface a top advisory strategy

v1.8 may not:
- execute a strategy
- authorize an action
- weaken governance
- mutate O43 memory directly
- interpret historical success as a guarantee of future success
