# O41 — Market Opportunity & Decision Quality

## Purpose
Promote market evidence into canonical opportunity candidates before Idea → Strategy, while preserving uncertainty and provenance.

## Changes
- Deterministic opportunity synthesis from market findings.
- Opportunity candidates carry problem, customer, outcome, solution format, assumptions, validation plan, scope, and provenance.
- Idea generation consumes opportunity candidates instead of converting source text directly into ideas.
- Idea → Strategy and Strategy → Product handoffs preserve opportunity identity and decision fields.
- Strategy scoring adds opportunity specificity.
- Backward-compatible theme-only generator path remains for legacy tests.

## Boundary
Candidates are hypotheses. Global signals still require Egypt-specific validation. The system does not claim profitability or product-market fit from generic web evidence.
